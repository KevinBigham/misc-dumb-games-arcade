Glaze

How it works

  • A
    Drag through touching tiles (diagonals count) to spell words of 3+ letters. Let go to fire it. Desktop: just type, Enter to fire.
  • 5
    5 letters leave an Ember tile. Use it in a word to burn its whole row.
  • 6
    6 letters leave a Glass tile: doubles the word it is in.
  • 7
    7+ letters leave a Gold tile (blasts 3×3) and throw a named pot onto your Shelf.
  • x
    Fire words within 4 seconds of each other to stoke the heat, up to x3.
  • !
    Clay rises from the firebox. If a stack is pushed past the crack line, the kiln splits.
