    How to swim

    Race craft in six walls

    1. Space
      The start

      "Take your mark." Wait for the beep, then dive. Move before it and you're disqualified.

    2. SpaceSpace
      Underwaters

      Off every wall you're streamlined and fast. Tap Space in rhythm to dolphin kick. It burns air, and past the 15-yard marks (pink floats) you're DQ'd. Hit an arm key to break out.

    3. ←→
      Freestyle

      Alternate arms. Wait for the meter to turn green before the next stroke; rushing slips water. Hold a steady tempo for 8 strokes to enter Flow (+5% power).

    4. Space
      Breathe

      Tap Space and your next stroke turns to air. Breath strokes are a little slower. Run out of air and your power fades.

    5. Space↓
      Flip turn

      Near the wall the action becomes FLIP. Hit it when your head is in the green band past the T. Too early and you float short; too late and you jam the wall.

    6. Space
      The finish

      On the last length, reach in the amber band to lunge for the pad. Glide in and you'll get out-touched.

    7. EscT[]
      Pacing

      Your Engine drains with every stroke and refills slowly. Sprint the 50. Pace the 200 and 500 or the bear finds you. T toggles a tempo trainer beep; [ ] set its tempo.

    On a phone: left and right pads are your arms, the middle pad is the action.
