How to Swim

Start

Wait for the beep, then hit ← or →. Go early and you're disqualified. One start, no mercy.

Stroke

Alternate ← → (or A D). Press when the needle hits green. Perfects build Flow, and Flow raises your tempo.

Breathe

↑ takes a breath on your next stroke. It costs a little speed. Running out of air costs a lot.

Kick

Hold Space. Big speed, burns your tank. A 50 can kick the whole way. A 200 can't.

Flip Turn

↓ when you're inside the green zone, 1–2 yards from the wall. Too early leaves you short; too late jams you.

Breakout

Off every wall, hold streamline. Take your first stroke when the speed needle drops into green. 15-yard limit.

Finish

Don't glide in. Hit ↓ inside the last 1.5 yards to reach for the pad.

On a phone

Use the big L and R pads, plus Kick (hold), Breathe, and Turn. Landscape gives you the most pool.
