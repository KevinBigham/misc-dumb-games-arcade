# Game #12 (GOAT Flagship): Chrono-Vortex: The God-Forge Arena

- **Genre:** Kinetic Pinball-Brawler & Time-Echo Bullet-Alchemist Roguelite
- **Primary Reference:** *Hades* + *Demon's Tilt / Peggle* + *Into the Breach*
- **Supporting Reference:** *Braid* (Temporal Echo Clones) + *Ikaruga* (Bullet Parry Alchemy)
- **One-Sentence Core Loop:** Ricochet your Striker and enemy automatons off 4 Elemental Resonance Bumpers inside an octagonal Cosmic Crucible, weave up to 3 parallel Chrono-Ghost Echoes of your own past 4 seconds, and parry enemy bullet-hell volleys into homing prism storms across 5 Sectors!
- **Short Goal:** Shatter the 4 Crucible Sectors, draft synergistic God-Forge Relics (or smelt them at the Anvil), and topple the 3-Phase Apex Boss **Kronos-Omega**.
- **Long Goal:** Master all 3 Striker Chassis (`VANGUARD-G`, `PHANTOM-X`, `PRISM-9`), unlock all 12 God-Forge Relics, and sustain a `16x` Pinball Overdrive Chain.
