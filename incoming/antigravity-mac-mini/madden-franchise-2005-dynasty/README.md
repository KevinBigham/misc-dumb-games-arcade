# 🏈 MADDEN FRANCHISE '05: DYNASTY EDITION

**A Stand-Alone Pro Football Franchise & Owner Mode Game Inspired by Madden NFL 2005**

Experience the golden era of football franchise management (`2004–2005` NFL season landscape led by cover athlete **Ray Lewis** and the **"Year of the Defense"**). Manage any of the **32 NFL Franchises** across a multi-season dynasty featuring **Storyline Central**, **The Tony Bruno Radio Show**, **Local & National Newspapers**, **Player Morale & In-Season Progression**, an **Interactive 11-on-11 2D Tactical Field & Playcaller** with the **2005 Hit Stick**, and a complete **5-Stage Offseason Pipeline**.

---

## 🌟 Signature Madden 2005 Franchise Features

1. **Storyline Central (New in Madden 2005)**:
   - **The Tony Bruno Show (EA Sports™ Radio)**: Weekly talk-radio segments with live fan call-ins, player interviews, studio bumper audio, and **Interactive Listener Polls** that impact **Team Morale**, **Fan Support %**, and **Owner Cash**.
   - **Local & National Newspapers (*USA TODAY Sports*)**: Weekly front-page beat reporting on your franchise's games, locker-room morale drama, and league-wide headlines.
   - **Coach's PDA Email Inbox**: Direct dispatches from your **Owner**, **Coordinators**, **Head Athletic Trainer**, **Scouts**, and **Player Agents** (playing-time complaints, holdout warnings, and extension requests).
2. **Player Morale & Dynamic In-Season Progression**:
   - Every player tracks **Morale (`0–100%`)** driven by depth chart role, offensive touches, contract status, and win/loss streaks.
   - **Weeks 4, 8, 12 & 16 In-Season Progression**: High-morale players and young stars develop `+1` to `+2` OVR mid-season under top-rated Coordinators.
3. **Interactive Game Day Broadcast & 2D Tactical Field**:
   - Call plays from a **12-play Offensive Playbook** and **10-play Defensive Playbook** or Instant-Sim any game/drive.
   - Use **2005 Defensive Playmaker Adjustments** (`Pinch D-Line`, `Jam / Press CBs`), Offensive Audibles (`Hot Route Star WR Streak`, `Max Protect`), and arm the **2005 HIT STICK** for bone-rattling tackles and forced fumbles on the animated 11-on-11 HTML5 Canvas field.
4. **Full Front-Office GM & Salary Cap Control (`$80.50M` 2004 Cap)**:
   - Reorder your **Depth Chart**, negotiate **In-Season Contract Extensions** with live Agent Interest feedback, apply the **1-Year Exclusive Franchise Tag**, sign **Free Agents**, or build **Multi-Asset Player + Draft Pick Trades** with CPU GMs before the **Week 8 Trade Deadline**.
5. **Owner Mode & Coaching Staff Clinic**:
   - Set stadium pricing for **Tickets**, **Concessions**, **Parking**, and **Marketing Budgets**, and invest Owner Cash to upgrade your **Head Coach**, **Offensive Coordinator**, **Defensive Coordinator**, and **Head Athletic Trainer**.
6. **5-Stage Offseason Dynasty Loop**:
   - **Stage 1**: Season Awards (MVP, DPOY, Best RB/WR) & Hall of Fame Retirements
   - **Stage 2**: Re-Sign Expiring Contracts & Exclusive Franchise Tag
   - **Stage 3**: Unrestricted Free Agency Frenzy
   - **Stage 4**: NCAA Scouting Combine (40-Yard Dash, Bench Reps, Vertical, Wonderlic) & 5-Round Live Rookie Draft
   - **Stage 5**: **Madden 2005 Training Camp Mini-Camp Drills** (*Hit Stick Alley*, *Pocket Presence*, *Oklahoma Trench Drill*, *Ballhawk Coverage*, *Clutch Kicking*) for permanent rating boosts!
