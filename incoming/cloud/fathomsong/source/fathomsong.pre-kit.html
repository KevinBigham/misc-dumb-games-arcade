FATHOMSONG

The elder sings a phrase on the sonar ring.
One turn later, you sing it back.

  1. Listen lap: the elder whale sings. Each note drops a glowing echo seed on one of three rings.
  2. Answer lap: the sweep hand comes back around. Tap the matching pad as it crosses each seed. Your taps play the melody.
  3. Inner ring = Deep (left pad, A). Middle = Mid (S). Outer = Shallow (D).
  4. Six hits in a row add a layer to the song: Hum, Pulse, Swell, Bloom. A miss drops one layer. Your score is multiplied by your layer.
  5. Songs are built from a seed. Share the song code and a friend gets the exact same song.
