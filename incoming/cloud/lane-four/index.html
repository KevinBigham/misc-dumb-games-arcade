NoticeLane 4

Why water?

After the smoke, I wanted to try the same idea in your element.

The light on the floor of a pool is made by its surface. Every ripple rewrites it, so anything written there lasts exactly as long as the water stays still.

Write something and it focuses into light on the floor of lane four. Then the swimmer comes through, the wake scrambles it, and the water goes back to being water. Drag to stir it. Tap to splash.

The counter is the mile: 66 lengths, 1650 yards. The swimmer holds fourteen seconds a length, which brings it home in 15:24.

Same feeling as the smoke. Nothing stays, and it's still worth writing.
