    How to swim

    Your body is the beat

    StartTap once to step up. Don't move on "Take your mark". Go on the beep. Early is a false start: Rookie and Club meets call you back, from Regional up it's a DQ. UnderTap either pad in rhythm to dolphin kick. Hold a pad to break out. You must be up by the 15 m mark. StrokeThe lit pad's ring closes when that arm is ready. Tap as it closes. Early taps are weak and burn energy. Late taps lose glide. ArmsFreestyle and backstroke: alternate left and right. Butterfly and breaststroke: both pads together. BreatheFreestyle and butterfly: hold a stroke to breathe. It costs a little glide. Run out of air and you gasp. PushThe Push pill shifts to a faster stroke rate. Faster, but it drains your tank. Empty tank = slow, heavy arms. WallsFreestyle and backstroke: tap both pads to flip as your head reaches the T on the floor. Butterfly and breaststroke: tap when your hands reach the wall. FinishTap within reach of the wall to lunge for the touchpad. Lunge when your arm is ready, not mid-stroke. KeysF / J or ← / → for left and right arms. Space = both. ↑ or W = push. P = pause.

    Flow: eight perfect strokes in a row and your stroke gets a little longer. Keep it going.
