NeonNightcap

Pitching rigs fill the night sky. Bat their pitches back at them. Hits put runners on base, and runners power up your swing.

How to hit

Tap anywhere to swing. Space or J works on a keyboard.

As the pitch arrives, the aim line sweeps from left field to right. Swing early to pull it left, late to go the other way. Tap when the line lights up a rig.

Pitches over the middle get CRUSHED. Pitches off the plate are weak, so lay off them: four balls is a walk.

Every runner on base adds +1 damage. Rigs still flying at your third out score for the other club. Win after 7 innings.
