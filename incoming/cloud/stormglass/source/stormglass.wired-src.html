STORMGLASS
drop skies · merge storms · don't overflow the jar

Drag to aim, release to drop. Two matching skies that touch merge into the next one up. Chain merges fast for combo multipliers.
Merges charge the ⚡ meter — a full meter hands you a Bolt that zaps the small junk it lands on. Gusts shove the whole pile sideways, so watch the right-side wind gauge.
Tap HOLD (or the NEXT box) to bank the current sky and swap it back later — one hold per drop, Bolts can't be held.
