SURVIVAL NOTES

  • Each run is five sectors. Survive the timer and a boss shows up. Beat the Vacuum 9000 to save the house.
  • Kills drop kibble crystals. Fill the XP bar to level up and pick an upgrade. Every level also makes you 3% stronger.
  • You get multiple lives. Losing one triggers a shockwave and a full heal. Buy more at the Scratching Post, up to all nine.
  • Gold-ringed elites drop loot crates with better-than-normal upgrades.
  • Chain kills for combos. Kills also charge ZOOMIES: seven seconds of invincible, triple-speed rampage.
  • You can hold four weapons. Max a weapon and own its partner upgrade to unlock an EVOLUTION.
