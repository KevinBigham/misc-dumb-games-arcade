# Playtest script (10 minutes)
1. Open the link on a phone. Tap New season. Read the scouting card and the coach's note without help.
   Watch for: do they understand fatigue 64 means "plays at 68%"?
2. Fixture 1: ask them to say out loud what they will do with the tired striker, then do it.
3. Turn on Math once during a match. Ask: "What decided possession 5?" Turn it off again.
4. Play to the recap. On a loss, ask: "Which one decision would you change?" Note whether they can name one.
5. Tap Replay season code, change only that decision, and see if the season changes.
6. Mid-season, reload the page (or close the tab and reopen). Confirm Continue season lands on the same screen
   and fatigue numbers did not jump.
Record: wins, time to finish, the decision they named, any button they tapped that did nothing.
