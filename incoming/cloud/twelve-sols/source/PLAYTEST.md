# 5-minute playtest script
1. Open the link, tap New run. Can you say the goal after 10 seconds?
2. Go out once, then open the Bench. Make Water Flask. Did you notice the hour bar drop?
3. In Experiment, pick Copper Wire + Scrap Plate (the hinted pair). Did "Discovered!" feel good?
4. Tap End Sol (twice). Read the night report. Do you understand why Hull dropped?
5. Play until the run ends. Afterwards: name one decision you'd change next run.
6. Start a second run. Did knowing recipes make Sol 1-3 feel different?
