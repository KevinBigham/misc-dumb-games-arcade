# Sporecrown — design contract (Round 1)

**One-sentence loop:** each turn you move a handful of units and pick what each fungal colony grows next, trading growth, chitin, lore and defense against three rival realms racing you on a tiny forest floor.

**Player fantasy:** steering a fungal realm from one Heartcap to the crown of the forest floor in about 15 minutes.

**Repeated decision:** what each colony makes next (Spore to expand, a fighter, a building, or the Great Bloom), and which side of each research fork to take. Every finished build forces a new pick, so most turns carry exactly one real tradeoff.

**Scarce resources:** Sap (growth), Chitin (production), Lore (research), and turns (40 total).

**Ways to win (any one ends the game):**
1. Conquest — hold 2 Heartcaps (yours and a rival's) through one full round, or be the last realm standing. The round of warning gives the loser one turn to strike back.
2. Great Bloom — research Sporefall (tier 4) and finish the 120-chitin Bloom. It grows at most 10 chitin a turn (carries in at most 20), gains nothing on a turn it is hit, loses 1.5x the damage of each hit, and defends at x0.7. Learning Sporefall alerts every rival.
3. Renown — highest Renown when turn 40 ends.

**Ways to lose:** your last colony falls, or a rival wins any of the three ways first.

**Two plausible strategies:** (a) wide and peaceful: spores early, lore groves, Sporefall, defend the Bloom; (b) tall and armed: Hard Shell, Thornswarm, Rot Siege, take capitals. A third, Renown-hoarding, sits between them.

**Twist:** Decomposition. Every unit that falls rots its tile into rich loam (+1 sap, max +2), so war fertilizes the land for whoever holds it. Plus forked research: each tier offers two techs and the other is lost forever, so a run becomes an identity.

**First sixty seconds:** title, one tap Play; your Heartcap, a Warden and a Raider are already on the map; a coach line says tap the Heartcap, choose a build, move the Raider, end turn. At turn 2 the first research fork opens.

**Unciv lessons used:** one authoritative turn pipeline (player, then each rival, then upkeep for all), a transparent ledger (yields and turns-to-finish shown on every option), production overflow capped at the item cost, blocked completions report a reason instead of vanishing, and rivals that act only through the same legal commands as the player.

**Realms (Round 3):** pick one on the title; rivals are the others. Amber Gill (Sun-gilled: +1 chitin per colony), Violet Veil (Driftspore: Spores cost 4 less and move 1 farther), Ash Morel (Ironstalk: fighters cost 2 less and hit 1 harder), Ruby Puffball (Quickflush: colonies need 2 less sap to grow). The daily realm fixes your realm from the date.

**Sieges (Round 2):** colony HP 12; Root Mesh x1.3 colony defense; a colony hit this turn does not heal; capturing plunders 6 + 2 per pop lore.

**Research costs:** tiers at 20 / 44 / 76 / 140 lore. Truce until turn 8 (no attacks).

**Outside this build:** diplomacy, trade, multiple maps, naval anything, online play, unit upgrades.
