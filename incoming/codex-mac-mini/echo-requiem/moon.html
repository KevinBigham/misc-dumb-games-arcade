A normal day at work.

Hostile clocks, overdue mail, and fish from accounting want you gone. You have toast. Make it their problem.

  1. Move: WASD or arrow keys. Hold mouse click to shoot toward your cursor. Hold Space to shoot at the nearest enemy. R toggles automatic aiming and shooting.
  2. Collect: enemies drop green teeth. Skate near them to pick them up.
  3. Pay rent: enter the moon’s dotted ring to deposit teeth. Meet the quota to choose a promotion. When the moon grows legs, follow the green rent arrow.
  4. Stay alive: Shift dashes through attacks. Collecting teeth charges your burp; press E at 100% to blast enemies and clear nearby bullets.
  5. Read the room: pink circles and dotted charge lines warn of danger. Move before they activate. Colored doors teleport you to their matching exit.
  6. Finish the job: pay three rent bills, then defeat the Sun from HR. Late rent costs one heart every 50 seconds. The boss has no deadline. Win to unlock optional, increasingly difficult overtime within that run.
GAMEPAD

Left stick moves. Right stick aims and shoots. A dashes. B burps. X or RT auto-aims when the right stick is idle. Start pauses. Use the mouse or keyboard for menus.

TOUCH

Drag the joystick to skate. Automatic toast is enabled. Tap DASH and BURP. Landscape gives you more room. You can turn auto-toasting off above the arena.

Esc or P pauses. 1 / 2 / 3 chooses a promotion. F toggles fullscreen. Sound and decorative motion can be reduced above the arena. Records and settings stay on this device; an unfinished run does not.
