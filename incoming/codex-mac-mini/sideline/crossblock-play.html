A LITTLE KNOW-HOW

Find your fit.

Pick one of the three shapes, then click a cell on the board to place it. You can also drag a shape onto the board.

  • Turn it around. Press R or Rotate to turn your selected shape. Rotation is always free.
  • Make a line. Fill all 8 cells in any row or column to clear it. Blocks stay put; there is no gravity.
  • Make a Cross. Clear a row and a column together for double line points.
  • Keep it going. Consecutive clearing moves build a streak. In Free play, use all three shapes to get a new hand.
  • Take your time. The game ends when none of your remaining shapes fits, even after rotation. Undo can take back your last placement.

Daily puzzle: Clear every block using just three shapes. Each day brings another solvable arrangement, based on your local calendar. Retry as often as you like. Your Free play board stays saved when you switch modes.

Your board and last undo save automatically in this browser. Free play and Daily puzzle have separate scores.

5 points per square placed · 100 × lines² per clear · Cross doubles line points · +50 per extra streak step · +500 for an empty board.

Keyboard: 1–3 choose · arrows move · R rotate · Enter / Space place · Z undo · M sound.
